title: Chat widget
description: A chat with your agent, in the corner of any website.
---

The widget adds a chat launcher and panel to your site, connected to one of your agents. It renders inside a Shadow DOM, so your page's CSS and the widget's never clash.

![Chat widget open on a page](/images/docs/widget-open.png)

## Before you start

- An [agent](/docs/concepts/agents/) to chat with.
- An [app](/docs/concepts/apps/) with your site in its allowed origins.
- Your site's origin in the server's `CORS_ORIGINS`.

## Who is chatting

The widget always chats as an end user of your app. Choose how it gets one:

- **Anonymous**: visitors chat without signing in. The widget handles it; you pass only the app id. See [anonymous users](/docs/auth/anonymous/).
- **Your users**: your backend issues a token for the signed-in user and you pass it to the widget. See [identify your users](/docs/auth/).
