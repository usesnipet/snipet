Snipet is an open-source platform to build AI agents that use your tools, and to put them in front of your users: in a chat widget on your site or through the API from your own product.

## How the pieces fit

```
Model ──┐
        ├──▶ Agent ──▶ App ──▶ Chat widget, or your own UI through the API
Plugins ┘
```

- **[Models](/docs/concepts/models/)**: connections to LLM providers, in the cloud or running on your hardware.
- **[Plugins](/docs/concepts/plugins/)**: connections to the services your agents can act on, from the catalog or any MCP server.
- **[Agents](/docs/concepts/agents/)**: a prompt, the models it runs on and the plugin actions it may call.
- **[Apps](/docs/concepts/apps/)**: where your users meet your agents. An app holds the API keys and decides who may chat.

## Where to start

- [Run Snipet](/docs/get-started/install/) on your machine or server.
- [Build your first agent](/docs/get-started/first-agent/) and try it in the playground.
- [Add the chat widget](/docs/widget/) to your site.
