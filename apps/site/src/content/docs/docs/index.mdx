---
title: Introduction
description: What Snipet is and how its pieces fit together.
---

Snipet lets you build AI agents, connect them to plugins and embed them in your apps.

- **Agents**: a system prompt, models and the plugins they may use.
- **Plugins**: connections to external services (MCP servers and more) whose actions agents can call.
- **Apps**: where agents are embedded, through the widget or the SDKs.
