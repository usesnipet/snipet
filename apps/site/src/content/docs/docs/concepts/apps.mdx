---
title: Apps
description: Where your users meet your agents.
---

An app is a place where your agents are used: your website, your product, a customer portal. It decides who can chat and from where. Every conversation of an end user belongs to an app.

Create apps in **Apps**.

![App form](/images/docs/apps-form.png)

## Allowed origins

The sites that may use the app from a browser, like `https://app.example.com`. Requests from other origins are rejected. Leave it empty if the app is only called from servers.

## Anonymous users

Turn on **Allow anonymous** to let visitors chat without signing in. The [chat widget](/docs/widget/) then creates an anonymous user per browser on its own. Anonymous access needs at least one allowed origin. See [anonymous users](/docs/auth/anonymous/).

## API keys

Your backend uses an app's API key to [issue tokens](/docs/auth/issue-tokens/) for your signed-in users. Create keys in **Api Key** and keep them on your servers.

![API keys page](/images/docs/api-keys.png)

## One app or several

Use one app per product or site. Separate apps keep their users, conversations and keys apart, so a key leaked from one can't act on another.
